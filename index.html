Motor Vehicle Accidents

Injured in an Accident? We Bill No-Fault Directly.

New York no-fault (PIP) insurance covers your medical care after a car accident — regardless of who was at fault. We evaluate your injuries and bill the insurer directly, so you can focus on recovery.

  • Referrals to specialists & imaging
  • Zero out-of-pocket cost to you
